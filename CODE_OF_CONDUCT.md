# Code of conduct

ICEBREAKER is a hobbyist, community-made project. Everyone is welcome, whatever
their experience, background or identity.

- Be kind and patient. Assume good faith; explain, don't mock.
- Critique code and ideas, never people.
- No harassment, discrimination, sexualised content, doxxing or threats —
  in issues, pull requests, modules, themes or anywhere the project lives.
- Respect people's time and their "no".

Maintainers may edit or remove content and block people who don't follow this.
Report a problem privately to the maintainers (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for
how to reach them); reports are handled confidentially.
