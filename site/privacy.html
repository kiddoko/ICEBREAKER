Privacy policy

ICEBREAKER desktop app · last updated 18 November 2026

ICEBREAKER is a desktop application that runs on your computer. It has no server and no account system run by its authors. We do not collect, receive, sell or share any of your data.

Google user data

If you choose to connect your Google account (CALENDAR module, cal google connect), ICEBREAKER asks Google for two permissions:

  • See your calendars and events (calendar.readonly): to list your calendars and show your events in the app.
  • View and edit events on your calendars (calendar.events): to add an event to the calendar you pick, and to remove an event, only when you ask for it.

How this data is handled:

  • ICEBREAKER talks directly from your computer to Google's servers (googleapis.com). The data never goes through a server of ours: there is none.
  • Events from roughly the last month and the next six months are kept in a local cache on your computer, so your agenda shows up offline. The cache is deleted when you disconnect.
  • The access token Google gives ICEBREAKER is stored only on your computer, in the app's configuration folder, readable by your user account only.
  • Google user data is not used for advertising, is not sold, is not shared with anyone, and is not used to train AI models. It is used only to show and change your calendar inside ICEBREAKER, at your request.
  • ICEBREAKER's optional AI assistant does not get access to your Google calendar data.

ICEBREAKER's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Run cal google disconnect in ICEBREAKER: it revokes the access at Google and deletes the token and the cached events from your computer. You can also remove ICEBREAKER at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Everything else

Your notes, journal, tasks, local calendar, settings and other module data stay in folders on your computer. Features that reach the internet do so only when you use them (weather, news, the AI assistant through the provider you configure, a one-time voice download), and send only what that feature needs. The voice is synthesized on your computer. Stealth mode turns every online feature off at once.
