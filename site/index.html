◆ ICEBREAKER

an open-source, terminal-styled cyberdeck for your desktop

ICEBREAKER is a desktop app (macOS, Windows, Linux) that turns a folder of Markdown notes into a keyboard-driven deck: notes, a daily journal, your own terminal, an AI assistant, and modules made by the community — each with its own shell command, app and home-screen widget.

CALENDAR and Google

The CALENDAR module shows your agenda in the app and lets you add events (cal add "dentist" fri 14:30), choosing which calendar they go to. It can work with a local calendar only, or with your Google calendars once you sign in with cal google connect.

  • Calendar data stays on your computer. There is no ICEBREAKER server.
  • ICEBREAKER only changes events when you ask it to.
  • cal google disconnect revokes access at any time.
